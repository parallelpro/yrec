## Possible bugs (not in bug_sweep)
1. Under SCV, R/mu does not match the density: compute_scv_envelope_eos and compute_scv_eos set rho, derivatives and ion fractions but not specific_gas_constant or electron_mean_weight_inverse (scv_eos.f90:306-405, 611-766); below the Saha ramp's foot R/mu stays Saha's (yale_eos.f90:207-210, no H2) while rho is SCV's; at the foot it jumps to beta P/(rho_SCV T) (blended_eos.f90:370-372). The pulse mu reads it (core/structure/structure_equations.f90:310-313). B, S.
2. SCV's general path reads an unbuilt column: compute_scv_eos (where M43 routes mismatched compositions) searches pressure in tablenv's column 1 (scv_eos.f90:549-588), filled only by the envelope build; before the first build every lookup is "outside" and silently keeps Saha; reached on the first card by set_new_last_shell_convection (starting_model.f90:199, before the build at 204). Fix: tablex(:,:,iscv_log10_p), identical after a build. B (small), S.
3. OPAL 1995/2001 trial calls compound stale slots: add_radiation and radsub run on the deriv_order = 1 trial calls (rad_flag = 1), rewriting cv (~x100 per call), E, S, chi and the gammas from stale slots (opal_eos.f90:796-798, 1342-1386; opal95_eos.f90:647-649, 815-855); the batch-0 fix comments (opal_eos.f90:787-790; opal95_eos.f90:635-638) cover only rad_flag = 0.

## eos -- summary

The eos domain is a five-way dispatch (`eos_get -> eos_eval -> meqos | eqstat -> eqstat2 -> {saha_eos, fully_ionized_eos} | {eqscve, eqscvg} | {oeqos, oeqos01, oeqos06}`) over ~50 files. The state modules (`opal_eos_lib`, `scv_eos_lib`, `yale_eos_lib`, `mhd_eos_lib`) and the small helpers (`mu`, `eos_mixture_lib`, `gmass*`, `quad*`) are in good shape. The dominant maintainability cost is triplication: the three OPAL vintages carry three copies of each of esac/rhoofp/oeqos/eqbound/readco/quad/t6rint/radsub/gmass, and `eqstat2` carries three ~60-line copies of the OPAL blend block; the MHD side carries an 11-way composition ladder (`mhdpx2`) plus 8 near-identical loader calls (`mhdst`) because state is stored as 11 named scalars rather than an indexed array. F77 artefacts (alternate returns `*999`/`return 1`, `-999` sentinels, `12345678` init flags, `continue` no-ops, goto-emulation blocks with dead tails, single-precision DATA) survive in nearly every OPAL file. Names are mostly good, but a handful are actively misleading (`atomic_weights` holds inverse weights; `species_mass_fraction` holds mole fractions; `helium_fraction_local` means two different things; `intent(inout)` on never-modified args). Four items below are not in ROADMAP 10/11: an untested 0/0 in the rhoofp01 priming path, an unused computed `t_fraction` in eqbound/eqbound01, a bounds check placed after the overflow in rabu, and `eos_get_gamma1` being dead in production.

## Structural recommendations (3-5)

1. One `opal_eos_vintage` derived type. Where: src/eos/opal_eos_lib.f90 (replace the ""/"_01"/"_06" member triples with a type holding tables, grids, index arrays, quad cache, `eos_output`, init flags, plus a small layout descriptor: nt, nvar, Z format, row format, has_amu_column). Mechanism: three instances `opal95, opal01, opal06`; convert quad*, t6rint*, eqbound/eqbound01, gmass*, and the rhoofp inversion to take the instance as an argument; keep esac*/readco* as thin vintage-specific wrappers until their remaining differences (06's amu/log10ne columns, esac06's radsub06 call) are also parameterized. Enables: deletes ~10 files, makes finding 1 above a single call, makes the rhoofp01 priming fix automatic. Cost: M-L, class A if done vintage by vintage with byte-compare after each.
2. Extract `blend_opal_result` from eqstat2 and route all vintage dispatch through one table-driven block. Where: src/eos/eqstat.f90:602-806. Mechanism: `subroutine blend_opal_result(eos_output, ramp, beta, ...)` plus a 3-element array of T6 gates; eqstat2 becomes one loop over enabled vintages. Enables: the beta14 convention discrepancy (known) becomes one line to fix; new vintages are data. Cost: M, class A.
3. Composition-indexed MHD state. Where: src/eos/mhd_eos_lib.f90 (11 named scalars/arrays -> arrays indexed by composition), mhdpx2.f90 (11-way ladder -> loop), mhdst.f90 (8 calls -> loop over a unit/path array). Mechanism: keep `rtab` sequence-association intact by passing contiguous slices. Enables: removes ~300 lines of ladder, makes the dead optional-centre logic obviously removable. Cost: M, class A.
4. Replace alternate returns and sentinels with status codes. Where: esac*/rhoofp*/oeqos*/eos_get_gamma1 and the SCV `valid_table_point` flag. Mechanism: `logical, intent(out) :: in_table` (or `ierr < 0` for the numerics-termination convention in GUIDELINES); rhoofp* return `in_table=.false.` instead of `-999`. Enables: removes every `*999`/`return 1`/`continue` no-op and the dead goto-emulation tails in esac*. Cost: M, class A.
5. Named indices for every packed array in the domain. Where: mhd_output(25) in mhd_eos_lib, OPAL eos_output slots in opal_eos_lib, SCV table columns in scv_eos_lib, the 12-species mixture order in eos_mixture_lib (fxenv/atomic_weights_full/saha_mass_fractions). Mechanism: `integer, parameter` blocks with one-line comments per slot. Enables: readers stop cross-referencing oeqos06:87-93 and gmass comments to decode `eos_output(4)`; makes findings 5, 6, 9, 10 trivial. Cost: S-M, class A.

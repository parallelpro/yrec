## mixing / setup / util / wind -- summary

- 36 files, ~7.9k lines, all read in full; nothing under /Applications/YREC was touched, no build or run.
- The area is in decent modern shape (free-form, intents, `ierr`), but three legacy patterns dominate the readability cost: (1) the `rates()`/`eqburn`/`dburn` "16 scalar copies of `xa` + 15 separate rate arrays" relay, repeated in mix, rotmix, timestep_limit_hburn/heburn and burn_mix_extrapolated; (2) the same 4-line "loop radiative shells one at a time, then each CZ as a block" iteration hand-written 4+ times; (3) `star%` and `rot_scr%` fields used as hidden side channels (rezone using `logP_start/logT_start/logRho_start/old_shell_mass` as spline scratch, rotmix stuffing `mix_scr%delm` into `star%gradT`, temperature_gradients reading `star%iovim` set elsewhere, timestep routines mutating `star%job%` flags).
- A whole sub-tree is dead: `burn_settle_mix` -> `burn_mix_extrapolated` is unreachable (`burs_extrapolation_active` is a hardcoded `.false.` local in evolve_angular_momentum.f90:104); `wcz` is documented dead. Several computed-but-never-read blocks exist inside live routines (rotation_stability_setup ZM98 block, rezone pmax1..5, massloss entropy loop, compute_scale_height pscap, timestep_limit_hr dt_scale).
- Duplicated physics blocks that should be single helpers: He-settling substep block (mix vs rotmix), CZ scan (find_convection_zones vs homogenize_convection_zones vs semiconvection merge), 4-point Lagrange interpolation x12 quantities x3 sites (rotation_stability_setup), wind torque/fixed-point skeleton (kawaler_wind vs matt_wind, wind_spindown vs wind_spindown_matt), log10 R from (L,Teff) in six wind files, dt-factor clamp tail in three timestep_limit_* files.
- Bugs: most of what I found is already in audit/bugsweep-*/ reports even though not in ROADMAP 10/11; genuinely new items are the always-zero `star%alfmlt/phmlt/cmxmlt` and `rot_scr%difad_shear_coeff2`, the kawaler_wind vs wind_spindown saturation-gate inconsistency, mdot's 1..11 species remix under extended composition, and rescale_model's swapped loop-variable names.

## Structural recommendations (3-5)

- Nuclear-rate relay as a single vector. Where: mix.f90, rotmix.f90, timestep_limit_hburn.f90, timestep_limit_heburn.f90, burn_mix_extrapolated.f90 (and rates()/eqburn/engeb signatures in burn_lib/net_lib). Mechanism: `rates()` writes `rate(1:n_rates)` for one zone; `rot_scr%reaction_rate_by_zone(k,:)` is filled by a slice assignment; `eqburn/engeb` take `composition(:,k)` and `rate(:)`. Enables: deleting ~250 lines of scalar copies, killing the unused rate_zero9/13 arrays, and making the burn-region iterator (next item) trivial. Cost: touches five callers and two lib signatures; arithmetic unchanged. Class A, effort M.
- Burn-region iterator. Where: mix.f90 (three loops), rotmix.f90, plus the CZ scan in find_convection_zones/homogenize_convection_zones/semiconvection. Mechanism: `build_burn_regions(convective_flag, mixed_zone_bounds, nz, regions)` producing an ordered list of (begin, end, is_convective) once per call; all four burn loops become `do r = 1, nregions; call solve_composition(regions(r)%begin, regions(r)%end, ...)`. Enables: one place to encode the "single-shell CZ is treated as radiative unless it is the centre" rule that is currently implicit in mix:382, homogenize:87 and find_convection_zones:91. Cost: careful ordering to preserve the exact sequence of solve_composition calls. Class A, effort M.
- Explicit scratch instead of `star%` side channels. Where: rezone.f90 (`*_start`/`old_shell_mass` as spline buffers), rotmix.f90 (`star%gradT` swap), temperature_gradients.f90 (`star%iovim`, and the always-zero `alfmlt/phmlt/cmxmlt`), compute_timestep.f90:84 and mdot.f90:293 (physics routines flipping `star%job%` flags). Mechanism: local buffers in rezone; gradient array argument for the settling routine; `zone_index` argument for temperature_gradients; return a "disable structure limits"/"disk exhausted" logical to the driver and let the driver flip the job flag. Enables: reading any of these routines without knowing what the previous Henyey pass left in `star`; makes the later "controls sanity checker" (section 8) possible because controls stop mutating mid-step. Cost: signature changes at ~8 call sites. Class A, effort M.
- One wind driver with a torque function. Where: kawaler_wind.f90 + matt_wind.f90 (substep + fixed-point solver) and wind_spindown.f90 + wind_spindown_matt.f90 (start/end torques). Mechanism: `wind_torque_rate(omega, params)` per law, shared `spin_down_cz(omega0, dt, I_cz, torque)` driver, shared `omega_saturation()` and `log10_radius_from_l_teff()`. Enables: fixing the kawaler_wind/wind_spindown saturation-gate inconsistency in one place, and removing six copies of the radius formula. Cost: modest; the PMM branch must keep its current `wind_loss_active` behaviour until the known item is resolved. Class A (refactor) / B (if the gate inconsistency is then unified), effort M.
- Delete the dead BS extrapolation path and the dead ZM98 tail. Where: burn_settle_mix.f90, burn_mix_extrapolated.f90, evolve_angular_momentum.f90:104,353-446, rotation_stability_setup.f90:458-475,479-480 and `difad_shear_coeff2` consumers in circulation_velocities.f90:235,249, wcz.f90. Mechanism: remove the files/branches, or move them to an `attic/` with a note in ROADMAP. Enables: ~700 fewer lines to read, and removes the only remaining sites of the format-inside-if and `write(*,10)` diagnostics. Cost: trivial for the code; a decision is needed on whether BS extrapolation is ever coming back. Class A, effort S.
